A matrix is rectangular array of values arranged in rows and columns. These values are
referred to as entries.

Matrices are subject to a variety of operations such as addition and multiplication.

A real matrix is a matrix with its entries as [[real numbers]], while a complex matrix has
[[complex numbers]] or a mixture of both complex and real numbers as its entries.
